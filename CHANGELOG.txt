Slot Machines - Changelog
========================

v1.2
----
- Completed the native reward-level audit and fixed additional empty payouts.
- Heavy ordnance starts at level 10, grenade/knife ordnance and Eridium at
  level 2, and repkits at level 3. Enhancements remain level 15; class mods 20.
- Weapon pools now respect their own requirements: assault rifles and
  shotguns at level 3, sniper rifles at level 5, pistols and SMGs from level 1.
- The same checks cover legendary/pearl weapons, jackpot loot, configured
  weapon pools and forced reward tests. Rarity odds and spin prices are unchanged
  except that Eridium results are unavailable to level-1 characters.

v1.1
----
- Fixed empty gear payouts on low-level characters caused by selecting
  equipment types that had not unlocked yet.
- Enhancements enter the reward mix at level 15; class mods enter at level 20.
  Earlier characters receive other available gear of the winning rarity.
- Applies to both editions, including the jackpot's guaranteed equipment
  and its random loot burst.
- Tests requesting a locked equipment type explain its required level
  before starting a spin.

v1.0
----
- Initial release.
